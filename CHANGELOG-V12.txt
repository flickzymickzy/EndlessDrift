ENDLESS DRIFT V12
=================
- The GARAGE tuning flow now defaults to the Performance tab for owned cars.
- Every performance upgrade has VIEW STATS and BUY buttons.
- BUY first opens the comparison/confirmation panel so no coins are deducted
  accidentally before the player confirms.
- 3D wheel rims moved onto the tire face, with spokes and hubs rotating in
  the same wheel group.
- Drift score popups moved to the very top of the view.
- Easier drift initiation and smoother left/right steering transitions.
- Police pursuit triggers at 100 KM/H (approx 62.14 MPH), not 100 MPH.
- Added COINS page with three example real-money Drift Coin packs.
  Payments are DISABLED until server-verified checkout and coin ledger work.
- Added ACCOUNT page with optional Supabase email/password auth.
- Added READ-ONLY global XP and coins-spent rankings from secure database.
  Client-side raw XP cannot update official rankings.
- Added backend/supabase-setup.sql and setup guides for accounts, ranks,
  payment provider, domain and online deployment.
- Preserved prior garage, cars, shop cosmetics, Drift Pass, police chase
  features, save key, tuning, levels, and local Drift Coin economy.
