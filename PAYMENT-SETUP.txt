ENDLESS DRIFT V11 — HOW TO CONNECT PAYMENTS SAFELY
==================================================

SHORT ANSWER TO "HOW DO I LINK MY CARD ACCOUNT?"

You should NOT directly link a personal credit/debit card inside the game.
For a real website you usually do this instead:

1. Create a verified Stripe BUSINESS account.
2. Add your PAYOUT BANK ACCOUNT inside Stripe Dashboard.
3. Create a Premium Pass product/price in Stripe.
4. Send players to Stripe Checkout.
5. Let Stripe handle the card form securely.
6. Your backend verifies the payment webhook.
7. Your backend marks that player account as Premium.
8. The game reads premium status from your backend.

So the safe answer is: connect Stripe (or a similar payment provider), then
connect your business payout bank account inside the provider dashboard.
Do not collect or store raw card details in your site code.

RECOMMENDED PATH FOR A UAE-BASED CREATOR

1. Prepare a legitimate UAE business and supporting verification documents.
   Stripe UAE generally requests current business documents and identity checks.

2. Open and verify a Stripe UAE account:
   https://stripe.com/ae

3. Add your payout bank account in Stripe Dashboard.
   Your earnings are paid out there. The customer's card is NOT your payout
   destination.

4. Create a Stripe product called:
   Endless Drift Premium Pass

5. Use Stripe Checkout with a secure backend.
   Required server flow:
   - Player clicks Buy Premium.
   - Your backend creates a Checkout Session using the SECRET key.
   - Player pays on Stripe-hosted checkout.
   - Stripe sends checkout.session.completed to your webhook.
   - Your backend verifies the webhook signature.
   - Your backend stores premium=true for that user account.
   - Your frontend loads premium status after login.

6. Add login/accounts BEFORE selling premium unlocks.
   Otherwise users can lose purchases when changing device/browser.

WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do not ask users to type card numbers into your game UI.
- Do not put Stripe secret keys in front-end JavaScript.
- Do not unlock premium from a success URL alone.
- Do not store paid status only in localStorage.
- Do not share bank or card details in ChatGPT.
